About IMPACT Martial Arts

IMPACT Martial Arts is a premium, multi-disciplinary martial arts organisation based in Switzerland. We combine a beginner-friendly atmosphere with professional coaching standards, structured curriculums and long-term athlete development. 

IMPACT is setting a new benchmark for martial arts in Switzerland:

High standards, clear structure, strong culture — delivered consistently across programmes and locations.

Our programmes include:

  • MMA
  • Muay Thai

  • Boxing

  • Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)

  • Wrestling

  • Kids & youth programmes

IMPACT provides the infrastructure strong coaches need:

  • Premium facilities and professional equipment

  • Structured curriculums and progression frameworks

  • A seamless digital member experience (app-based training journey)

  • Clear coaching standards across disciplines

  • A strong, respectful team culture

  • Long-term thinking and professional operations



This environment allows coaches to lead effectively, develop hobbyists over time, and contribute to building a high-performance fight team within a stable system.

  • Maintain coaching standards across classes and programmes

  • Coach beginners, advanced hobby athletes and competitive fighters alike

  • Work closely with Head coach and other coaches
