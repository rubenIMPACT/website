Terms and Conditions Little Ninjas

1. Scope and Amendments of the GTC

These General Terms and Conditions (GTC) govern the legal relationship between the legal guardian of the minor member ("Little Ninja") and IMPACT Martial Arts AG, Walchestrasse 15, 8006 Zurich, Switzerland (hereinafter referred to as "IMPACT"). They apply to all Little Ninjas programs, services, and memberships offered by IMPACT.

IMPACT reserves the right to amend these GTC at any time. Amendments will be communicated to the legal guardians in a timely and appropriate manner. Unless the legal guardian objects in writing within thirty (30) days of notification, the amendments shall be deemed accepted.

The contract is concluded by the legal guardian, who confirms that they have full legal authority to represent and legally bind the minor to these GTC. The legal guardian is responsible for ensuring that the minor complies with all provisions of this agreement.

The legal guardian confirms that the child is in good physical condition and medically cleared to participate in martial arts training. Any known health conditions or limitations must be disclosed to IMPACT prior to participation. IMPACT reserves the right to request a pediatrician's medical certificate in individual cases.

2. Membership and Services

IMPACT offers supervised group martial arts training programs for children under the Little Ninjas brand. The scope of services and applicable prices are determined by the selected membership package as defined in the current membership and price overview valid at the time of contract conclusion.

Memberships are personal and non-transferable. IMPACT reserves the right to modify training schedules, class formats, trainers, and studio locations at any time. There is no entitlement to a specific trainer, class time, or location.

If a comparable studio within a reasonable radius of five (5) kilometers is provided, this shall be deemed equivalent. If no such substitute location is available, the legal guardian may terminate the membership without penalty.

Operational changes due to organizational reasons, safety considerations, or force majeure do not entitle members to refunds, replacement sessions, or compensation. Contractual payment obligations remain unaffected.

3. Membership Packages, Contract Duration, Termination, and Renewal

Membership Packages

IMPACT offers the following Little Ninjas membership packages:

  • NINJA BASIC – 1 training session per week
  • NINJA CORE – 2 training sessions per week

Available contract durations and payment modalities (monthly or annual) are defined in the current membership and price overview.

Termination and Renewal

Memberships may be terminated at the end of the agreed contract term with a notice period of one (1) month. If no termination is received within the notice period, the membership automatically renews for the originally agreed contract duration.

Termination requests must be submitted in writing via email to support@impact-martialarts.com.

4. Contract Pausing and Extraordinary Termination

Extraordinary Pause for Medical Reasons (Minimum One Month)

A temporary pause of the membership is permitted only in cases of severe illness or injury affecting the child.

Conditions:

  • Minimum pause duration: one (1) full month
  • Submission of a valid medical certificate from a licensed physician specifying start and end dates
  • Request must be submitted in writing before the pause begins
  • Retroactive pauses are excluded

During an approved pause, payment obligations are suspended and the contract term is extended accordingly.

Extraordinary Termination (Only Valid After 6 Months)

Extraordinary termination of the membership is only possible after a minimum active contract duration of six (6) months.

Extraordinary termination may be granted in the following cases, provided the expected duration exceeds six (6) months:

  • Long-term medical conditions
  • Relocation or extended stay abroad

All extraordinary termination requests must be submitted in writing and supported by appropriate documentation. IMPACT reserves the right to review and verify all submitted documents.

Additional Extraordinary Termination Options

Extraordinary termination is possible for other valid reasons such as pregnancy, military service, extended stay abroad, relocation, or further education, provided the expected duration exceeds six (6) months. A written request, along with supporting documentation specifying start and end dates, must be submitted via email to support@impact-martialarts.com.

No Pro-Rata Refunds / Credits (Billing Period)

In the event of Extraordinary Termination, the membership remains valid until the end of the current billing period. The billing period depends on the selected payment plan:

  • For monthly memberships, the billing period is one (1) month, starting on the membership billing date (date of the monthly payment), and ending the day before the next billing date.
  • For yearly memberships, the billing period is twelve (12) months or twenty-four (24) months, starting on the membership billing date (date of the yearly payment), and ending the day before the next annual or biennial billing date.

No pro-rata refunds, partial-period reimbursements, or credits will be granted for unused training time during a running billing period, whether monthly or yearly.

This also applies in the case of membership pauses: retroactive pauses are excluded and do not entitle the member to any credit or refund.

No Entitlement for Short-Term Absences

There is no entitlement to pause, reduce, or terminate the membership for absences shorter than one (1) month, including but not limited to:

  • Short-term illness or injury
  • Holidays or travel
  • Personal, school-related, or scheduling reasons

Absences of one (1) to two (2) weeks do not qualify for any contractual adjustment.

Liability in Case of Operational Interruptions

IMPACT is not liable for non-performance due to force majeure (e.g., natural disasters, pandemics). In the event of a closure exceeding one month, the contract term is extended accordingly.

5. Starter Package

Upon initial enrollment, the purchase of a Little Ninjas Starter Package is mandatory.

The starter package includes the required training outfit ("Gi") and initial onboarding services. The starter package fee is charged as a one-time payment according to the current price overview and is non-refundable.

6. Upgrades and Downgrades

a) Upgrades

Upgrades to a higher membership package are possible at any time. In such cases, the existing contract is terminated and replaced by a new contract. Any payments already made will be credited on a pro-rata basis.

  • Procedure: Termination of the current contract. Immediate commencement of the new contract.
  • Financial Implications: Settlement of remaining obligations. New membership fees apply from the start date of the new contract.

b) Downgrades

Downgrades are only possible at the end of the current contract term and must be requested in writing within the applicable notice period.

7. Payment Defaults and Debt Collection Costs

Membership fees and all other amounts owed to IMPACT are due on the billing date. If a payment is not received by the due date, the member is considered to be in default.

If a payment remains unpaid after IMPACT has sent at least one written payment reminder to the email address or mobile number provided by the member, and the member remains in default, IMPACT may initiate formal debt collection proceedings. In such cases, a flat debt collection fee of CHF 100 will be charged to the member and added to the outstanding amount. The member is responsible for this fee in addition to the outstanding contractual amount, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

8. Participation in Training

The legal guardian confirms that the child is physically fit to participate in sports activities. Participation is prohibited in cases of illness symptoms or officially ordered quarantine.

Training Attendance

  • NINJA BASIC / NINJA CORE: Participation is flexible within the booked number of weekly sessions. Unused training sessions expire at the end of each calendar week.

Holidays and Public Holidays

IMPACT is closed during calendar weeks 31, 52, and 1 and offers reduced operations during calendar week 32.

On public holidays officially recognized in the Canton of Zurich, IMPACT is closed. There is no entitlement to compensation, replacement training, or extension of the membership term due to these closures.

9. Audio, Image, and Video Recordings

Audio, image, and video recordings may be made during training sessions for internal documentation and marketing purposes (e.g. website and social media).

Legal guardians may object to recordings involving their child by submitting a written objection at the time of contract conclusion. In such cases, no recordings of the child will be made or used.

10. Communication

All relevant information regarding training schedules, changes, or emergencies will be communicated to the legal guardian using the provided contact details. The legal guardian is responsible for keeping this information accurate and up to date.

11. Liability and Insurance

Participation in Little Ninjas training is at the child's own risk. The legal guardian is responsible for ensuring adequate accident and liability insurance coverage.

To the extent permitted by law, IMPACT excludes liability for injuries, damages, or loss of personal belongings, valuables, or clothing.

12. Authority to Issue Instructions and House Rules

The child is required to follow all staff instructions and comply with the applicable house rules. In cases of gross or repeated violations, IMPACT reserves the right to terminate the contract without notice. No refund entitlement exists in such cases.

13. Data Protection

Personal data is processed in accordance with IMPACT's Privacy Policy available at www.impact-martialarts.com/en/privacy.

14. Applicable Law and Jurisdiction

These GTC are governed exclusively by Swiss substantive law. The exclusive place of jurisdiction for all disputes arising from or in connection with these GTC is Zurich, Switzerland.
