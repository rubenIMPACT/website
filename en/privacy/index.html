3. Cookies and Tracking in connection with the use of the IMPACT website

A) Server log files

The website automatically saves some of the information usually transmitted by the browser in its server log files, insofar as your browser provides this information. This data is not merged with other personal data.

B) Cookies and tracking

When you visit our website, IMPACT occasionally uses cookies, i.e. small text files that are stored on your computer and saved by your browser. They are used to make our website user-friendly, effective and secure. You may refuse the use of cookies by selecting the appropriate settings on your browser, however please note that if you do this you may not be able to use the full functionality of this website.

IMPACT sometimes uses Google Analytics or similar services on its website. This is a service provided by third parties, which may be located in any country in the world (in the case of Google Analytics, it is Google LLC in the USA, www.google.com), with which IMPACT can measure and evaluate the use of the website (not on a personal basis). Permanent cookies set by the service provider are also used for this purpose.The service provider does not receive any personal data from IMPACT, but can track your use of the website, combine this information with data from other websites that you have visited and which are also tracked by the service provider, and use these findings for its own purposes (e.g. control of advertising).If you have registered with the service provider yourself, the service provider also knows you. The processing of your personal data by the service provider is then the responsibility of the service provider in accordance with its data protection provisions.The service provider only informs IMPACT how its website is used (no information about you personally).
