About us

Our IMPACT.

IMPACT Martial Arts stands for radical transformation. For growth. For real, lasting change. Everything we do is designed to empower you: physically, mentally and emotionally. In Zurich and Winterthur. So you can shape your life the way you truly want it.

The Story Behind.

Our community's roots go back to 2021, when Samuel S. Bauer founded the ICCS Sports Academy, a school fully focused on modern self-defense. His mission: to give people in Zurich a new sense of confidence, especially in moments when they feel unsure or powerless.

Then came summer 2023 and with it a game-changing encounter. Sam met Ruben Crawford, a former professional MMA fighter. Two very different backgrounds, but one shared mission: to teach martial arts in a supportive, empowering environment and help people become truly strong.

From this fusion, IMPACT Martial Arts was born. More than just a gym, more than just a studio. A home for anyone who wants to grow. A safe place to develop strength. A community where no one fights alone.

Sam & Ruben, the founders
