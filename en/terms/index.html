3. Memberships ( BASIC, CORE, ADVANCED, PRO)

IMPACT offers the following memberships: IMPACT BASIC (1 Training per Week), IMPACT CORE (2 Trainings per Week), IMPACT ADVANCED (3 Trainings per Week), and IMPACT PRO (Unlimited Trainings per Week), each available with minimum terms of twelve (12) or twenty-four (24) months.
Payment can be made monthly, annually, or biannually.

Termination and Renewal:

  • 12- and 24-Month Terms: Termination at the end of the agreed term with a notice period of one month. If no timely termination is received, the membership automatically renews for the original term.

Terminations must be made in writing via email to support@impact-martialarts.com.

Contract Pausing or Termination:

Extraordinary Pause for Medical Reasons (Minimum One Month)
Members may request a pause to their membership only in cases of severe injury or illness. The pause must be for a minimum duration of one (1) month and requires the submission of a valid medical certificate from a licensed physician, specifying the start and end dates of the absence. Requests must be submitted in writing via email to support@impact-martialarts.com before the pause begins. A retroactive pause is not possible. During the pause, payment obligations are suspended, and the membership term is extended accordingly.

No Entitlement for Short-Term or Discretionary Absences

There is no entitlement to pause or terminate the membership for absences shorter than one (1) month, including due to illness, injury, travel, or personal reasons.

No Pro-Rata Refunds / Credits (Billing Period)

In the event of Extraordinary Termination, the membership remains valid until the end of the current billing period. The billing period depends on the selected payment plan:

  • For monthly memberships, the billing period is one (1) month, starting on the membership billing date (date of the monthly payment), and ending the day before the next billing date.

  • For yearly memberships, the billing period is twelve (12) months or twenty-four (24) months, starting on the membership billing date (date of the yearly payment), and ending the day before the next annual or biennial billing date.

No pro-rata refunds, partial-period reimbursements, or credits will be granted for unused training time during a running billing period, whether monthly or yearly.

This also applies in the case of membership pauses: retroactive pauses are excluded and do not entitle the member to any credit or refund.

Extraordinary Termination for Long-Term Medical Conditions (Over Six Months)

If a member sustains a medical condition expected to last more than six (6) months, the membership may be terminated extraordinarily upon receipt and verification of medical documentation from a licensed physician.

Additional Extraordinary Termination Options

Extraordinary termination is possible for other valid reasons such as pregnancy, military service, extended stay abroad, relocation, or further education, provided the expected duration exceeds six (6) months. A written request, along with supporting documentation specifying start and end dates, must be submitted via email to support@impact-martialarts.com.

Liability in Case of Operational Interruptions

IMPACT is not liable for non-performance due to force majeure (e.g., natural disasters, pandemics). In the event of a closure exceeding one month, the contract term is extended accordingly.
